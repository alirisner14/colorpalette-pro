# Getting started with Color Palette PRO

> Use this text in the purchase confirmation email, on the download/access
> page, and as `READ-ME-FIRST.txt` if you deliver the app's files. Buyers who
> know where their palettes are saved and how to back them up don't lose them.

---

## Open it

Open the link from your order email in a current browser: Safari, Chrome,
Edge or Firefox. That's it — there is nothing to install and no account to
create. The first time, keep the page open for a few seconds while you are
online; the app stores itself on your device.

## Add it to your home screen (recommended)

It then opens like any other app, full screen, and works **offline**.

- **iPhone / iPad (Safari):** tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** tap **⋮** → **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
- **Windows / Mac (Chrome or Edge):** click the **install** icon at the right
  of the address bar, or **⋮** → **Install Color Palette PRO**.

## It works with no internet

Once the app has loaded one time, you can use all of it with no connection:
making palettes, your swatch book, example artwork, printing and exporting.
The gear icon (**Settings**) shows **Works offline** when it is ready, and
what is stored on your device. When an update is available you will see a
message with an **Update** button.

## What you can do

- **Make palettes** from a color, a photo, a mood (type "rainy café") or a
  theme, or build your own. Lock the colors you love and shuffle the rest.
- **Save them in your swatch book**, shown as a **flipbook** or as a **fan
  deck**. Choose **Customize** to pick the shapes, what to show, how many
  palettes go on a page or blade, whether to add example artwork, and to
  dress up the **cover** with a color or a picture and your own words.
- **See a palette on example artwork** (a mandala, a room, a web page and
  more) and check text contrast.
- **Print and cut** swatch decks, books and cards for matching or swatching
  your own paints, pencils and markers (PDF, SVG, PNG or JPG). Print at 100%
  size. For a cutting machine, use the SVG "cut lines only" file, or the
  transparent PNG for Print Then Cut.
- **Share** a palette as a link, a short code or a QR code.
- **Import** palette files from other apps, and **export** to them.

## Where your palettes are saved

Your swatch book is saved **in this browser, on this device**. Nothing is
uploaded, so:

- It won't appear on your other devices or in other browsers.
- **Clearing this site's data, browsing in a private window, or using the
  delete options in Settings removes it.**
- Uninstalling the home-screen app removes it.

**Back it up.** Open **Swatch Book › More › Back up my swatch book** (or
**Settings**). You get two choices:

- **Flipbook page (.html)** opens in any browser with no internet and turns
  its pages (or fans its blades) just like your swatch book. It also holds
  everything needed to restore.
- **Data file (.json)** is a small file made just for restoring.

Keep a backup somewhere safe, such as a cloud drive, an email to yourself or a
USB stick. To bring it back, choose **Restore from a backup** and pick the
file. You can replace your swatch book or add the backup's palettes to it.

You can also export single palettes: open any palette and use **Quick
export**: Procreate, Adobe & Affinity, Photoshop, GIMP/Krita, Figma & Canva,
images, CSS and more.

## Importing into your favorite apps

| App | Export | How to import |
|---|---|---|
| Procreate | `.swatches` | Open the file on your iPad, or drag it onto the Palettes panel |
| Photoshop | `.aco` or `.ase` | Swatches panel → ☰ → **Import Swatches** |
| Illustrator / InDesign | `.ase` | Swatches panel → ☰ → **Open Swatch Library → Other Library** |
| Affinity Designer / Photo | `.ase` | Swatches panel → ☰ → **Import Palette** |
| GIMP / Krita / Inkscape | `.gpl` | Palettes → **Import**, or copy into your palettes folder |
| Figma / Canva | `.svg` | Drag the swatch sheet onto your canvas; use the eyedropper |
| Canva Brand Kit | Hex codes | Paste the copied codes into your brand colors |

Menu names vary between app versions.

## System requirements

- A current version of Safari (iOS/iPadOS/macOS 16 or later), Chrome, Edge or
  Firefox
- About 3 MB of storage, plus room for your swatch book and any cover pictures
- **Pick from screen** works in Chrome and Edge on desktop computers. Other
  browsers offer **Pick from image** instead (take a screenshot, then pick).
- Offline use needs a browser that allows offline apps, and the app opened
  from its web address (not as a file).
