# Getting started with Color Palette PRO

> Use this text in the purchase confirmation email, on the download/access
> page, and as `READ-ME-FIRST.txt` if you deliver the app's files. Buyers who
> know where their palettes are saved don't lose them.

---

## Open it

Open the link from your order email in a current browser: Safari, Chrome,
Edge or Firefox. That's it — there is nothing to install and no account to
create.

## Add it to your home screen (recommended)

It then opens like any other app, full screen, and works **offline**.

- **iPhone / iPad (Safari):** tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** tap **⋮** → **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
- **Windows / Mac (Chrome or Edge):** click the **install** icon at the right
  of the address bar, or **⋮** → **Install Color Palette PRO**.

## Where your palettes are saved

Your swatch book is saved **in this browser, on this device**. Nothing is
uploaded, so:

- It won't appear on your other devices or in other browsers.
- **Clearing this site's data, or browsing in a private window, removes it.**
- Uninstalling the home-screen app removes it.

**Export palettes you love.** Open any palette and use **Quick export**:
Procreate, Adobe & Affinity, Photoshop, GIMP/Krita, Figma & Canva, images,
CSS and more.

## Importing into your favorite apps

| App | Export | How to import |
|---|---|---|
| Procreate | `.swatches` | Open the file on your iPad, or drag it onto the Palettes panel |
| Photoshop | `.aco` or `.ase` | Swatches panel → ☰ → **Import Swatches** |
| Illustrator / InDesign | `.ase` | Swatches panel → ☰ → **Open Swatch Library → Other Library** |
| Affinity Designer / Photo | `.ase` | Swatches panel → ☰ → **Import Palette** |
| GIMP / Krita / Inkscape | `.gpl` | Palettes → **Import**, or copy into your palettes folder |
| Figma / Canva | `.svg` | Drag the swatch sheet onto your canvas; use the eyedropper |
| Canva Brand Kit | Hex codes | Paste the copied codes into your brand colors |

Menu names vary between app versions.

## System requirements

- A current version of Safari (iOS/iPadOS/macOS 16 or later), Chrome, Edge or
  Firefox
- About 2 MB of storage
- **Pick from screen** works in Chrome and Edge on desktop computers. Other
  browsers offer **Pick from image** instead (take a screenshot, then pick).
