## The short version

**Color Palette PRO is delivered immediately and all sales are final.** We
make exceptions where the law requires one, and where the app genuinely does
not work for you and we cannot fix it. Talk to us before asking for your
money back — almost every problem is solvable, usually the same day.

## 1. Why sales are final

You receive the complete, working app the moment you pay. There is nothing to
return, and nothing we can take back. A refund on digital software means you
keep the product and we return the payment, which is why the default across
the industry is that sales are final.

## 2. When we will refund you anyway

**2.1 It does not work and we cannot fix it.** If the app will not run in a
supported browser on a device that meets the stated requirements, and we
cannot resolve it with you within a reasonable time, we will refund you in
full. **Give us the chance first** — tell us your device and browser and send
a screenshot, and we will usually have an answer within a day.

**2.2 You were charged twice**, or charged the wrong amount. Refunded in full
without question.

**2.3 You never received it.** If delivery failed and we cannot get the
product to you, you get your money back.

**2.4 Within 14 days in the EU or UK**, where you have not waived the right —
see section 4.

**2.5 Where consumer law otherwise requires it.** Your statutory rights are
not affected by anything in this policy.

## 3. When we will not

**3.1 You changed your mind**, or bought it by accident, after accessing it.

**3.2 You did not read what it does.** The product page and screenshots
describe the app accurately. Check before buying, and ask us anything
beforehand — we would rather answer ten questions than process one refund.

**3.3 Another app changed what it imports.** Color Palette PRO exports files
for apps made by other companies, who can change their formats. That is a
limitation we state plainly before you buy; it is not a defect. Tell us and
we will try to update the export.

**3.4 An optional feature is not available in your browser** — for example,
picking a color from the screen, which only some browsers support. The app
offers an alternative where a feature is missing.

**3.5 You lost your swatch book** by clearing your browser data, using a
private window, or uninstalling the app. Palettes are stored on your device,
and the app tells you to export anything you want to keep.

**3.6 You want it to do something it does not do.** Feature requests are
welcome. They are not grounds for a refund.

**3.7 You breached the licence** — shared your copy or access, redistributed
the app, or used it in a way the EULA forbids.

## 4. EU and UK buyers

If you are a consumer in the EU or UK you normally have **14 days** to cancel
a distance purchase.

That right does not apply to digital content you have consented to receive
immediately. **At checkout you will be asked to tick a box** confirming that
you want immediate access and understand you are giving up the right to
cancel. If you tick it and open the app, the 14-day right no longer applies.

Prefer to keep it? Do not tick the box. Email ontherisedigital@gmail.com and
we will hold delivery for 14 days instead.

## 5. How to ask

Email **ontherisedigital@gmail.com** with:

- the email address you bought with
- the order number or approximate purchase date
- what went wrong

We reply within **2 business days** and process approved refunds within
**5 business days**. The money returns by the method you paid with; your
bank may take several days longer to show it.

## 6. Chargebacks

Please come to us first. A chargeback costs us the sale and a fee on top, and
takes weeks to resolve, where an email usually takes hours.

We contest chargebacks where the product was delivered as described and we
were given no chance to help. We may decline future sales to accounts that
charge back without contacting us.
